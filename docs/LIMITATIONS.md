# Limitations and remaining research work

1. **Tactical identification:** no assigned strategies or randomized interventions exist in the selected data. All tactic effects, planned allocations, resistance scenarios and effect distributions are assumptions. Policy rankings are conditional calculations, not coaching prescriptions.
2. **Baseline evidence:** the default cutoff's held-out Brier advantage does not clear the declared support gate. It remains exploratory. Calibration is descriptive and may be weak in sparse bins. More historical validation, rolling evaluation and defensible calibration are needed before using it as a reliable forecast.
3. **As-was information:** strict event dates prevent target/future fight leakage, but the current archive may contain later corrections. True publication-time availability cannot be reconstructed from this snapshot.
4. **Source authority:** archive hashes, row consistency and provenance are checked. A complete primary UFCStats reconciliation is outstanding; a direct default-bout check returned a gateway error. Source-site rights are not independently established. The data are local and excluded from Git.
5. **Identity and outcome selection:** ambiguous names, duplicate source records, unsupported early formats, nonbinary outcomes and DQs are excluded. This changes the population. Retained win/loss history is not the full UFC/professional record.
6. **Prediction design:** no age, reach, stance, division-specific effects, individual round-format response, recency weighting, opponent strength, injury, preparation, camp or judging model. These were not invented from current-career snapshots.
7. **Uncertainty:** iid bout bootstrap ignores repeated-fighter dependence and fixes the selected regularization/feature histories. Structural draws assume independence of baseline, s, g and response weights, apart from Dirichlet simplex dependence. Separate SDs are not an additive variance decomposition.
8. **Feasibility:** planned policy allocations do not guarantee fight-state time. Small takedown samples trigger a warning but do not establish an athlete's actual physical or coaching feasibility.
9. **Robustness:** default stability is assessed over a finite grappling-effect × resistance grid plus paired simulation intervals. It is not a certificate over all possible model structures or expert-elicited ranges. The 0.01 utility margin and support rule are declared engineering choices.
10. **Engineering scope:** local research beta, no production deployment, authenticated multi-user persistence or externally audited dependency security certification. Uploaded files are bounded CSVs processed in memory; only the documented archive schema is accepted. Controlled application errors are understandable; unexpected runtime errors are hidden from visitors and remain in development logs.

Next research priorities: verify source records/rights, improve temporal forecast evidence and calibration, address dependence/sparse-history uncertainty, elicit defensible tactic ranges and feasibility constraints, then reassess robustness. More simulations alone cannot resolve these issues.
